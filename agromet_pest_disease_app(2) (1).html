🎯 1. Threat Score Calculation (0 to 12 Scale)

The threat score measures environmental & crop susceptibility on a 12-point scale:

  • Max Temp (Tmax): +2 pts (optimal range), +1 pt (borderline)
  • Min Temp (Tmin): +2 pts (optimal night range), +1 pt (borderline)
  • Relative Humidity (RH): +2 pts (high canopy wetness), +1 pt (moderate)
  • Rainfall (Rain): +2 pts (conducive accumulation), +1 pt (light rain)
  • Dew Proxy Index: +2 pts (HIGH / VERY HIGH dew), +1 pt (MID dew)
  • Wind Speed & Cloud Cover: +1 pt each if calm & overcast
  • Crop Stage Susceptibility: +1 pt if crop is in sensitive growth stage
⚠️ Biological Heat/Drought Cap: Fungal/bacterial disease scores are automatically capped at ≤ 5/12 (LOW/MEDIUM) if Tmax > 36°C or RH < 55%.

🌫️ 2. Dew Proxy Index Calculation

Dewfall proxy measures nocturnal leaf wetness duration:

  • NIL: RH < 60% and Rainfall < 0.5 mm
  • LOW: RH 60% - 74%
  • MID: RH 75% - 87% or Rain ≥ 0.5 mm
  • HIGH: RH ≥ 88% or (RH ≥ 80% with Rain ≥ 2 mm)
  • VERY HIGH: RH ≥ 94% or (RH ≥ 88% with Rain ≥ 5 mm)
💡 Nocturnal Dew Boosts: Dew level increases by +1 step if Tmin ≤ 24°C, Wind ≤ 6 km/h, or Cloud Cover ≥ 6 octa (overcast night retention).
